# Surface brief: Inicio del admin (/admin/dashboard)

Mode: Operate. Visitor: the owner of So Beauty, on a phone between clients and on a computer at the desk. Task: see who is coming today and what is next, and how the month is going. Constraints: Spanish with "vos"; the shell, navigation and controls stay standard; phone first, desktop equal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm spa treatment menu for the day: what is next, then the day, then the month, in soft pastels, refusing the grid of equal statistic tiles that every appointment panel opens with.
OWN-WORLD: Sage (#DCE8DC) top bar and rail, lilac (#E6DFF1) for the next turn and the active item, peach (#FBE3D6) for pending, on a pale sage ground (#F4F7F3); plum ink (#3B2A4A) and a deeper plum (#5B3F73) for primary actions; Nunito Sans at weights 400 to 800; large rounded cards with no shadows, thin sage rules between rows, pills for state.
STORY: The owner opens the app and knows, without scrolling, who is next and in how many minutes, sees the rest of the day in time order with each state, and reads the month's numbers underneath. She can open Turnos or Calendario in one tap.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: sage top bar with the name and the menu button; "Próximo turno" label, a lilac card with the time at 56px weight 800, the client name and the service; below it "Hoy" with a time-ordered list of rows (time, client and service, state pill); the month's two key numbers under the list; a bottom bar with Inicio, Turnos, Calendario and Más (opens the full menu). Desktop: the same content in two columns, next turn and list on the left, month on the right, with the sage rail on the left.
FORM: Carta de spa, offered as a grounded candidate in the safer round of the roll; seed:f9a9fbe8be94c7cb.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
